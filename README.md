# Stick Fight

A 1v1 ragdoll stickman duel game for phones, in one HTML file. Physics-based fighters, random weapons with abilities, a Smash-style knockback system, and landscape play.

**Play:** https://zanymany.github.io/Stick-Fight/

## Install it like an app (iPhone)
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch it from the home-screen icon — it runs full-screen with no browser bar.

## Controls
- **Fixed joystick** in the bottom-right (2P: P1 bottom-left, P2 bottom-right). Direction is movement *and* where your weapon points.
- **Let go** = use your weapon's ability (cooldown shown on your card). Melee weapons also hurt just by hitting.
- **Hold up** = jump (works mid-air when the cooldown is ready). **Push down** = drop through platforms.
- 2 players on one phone: left half is P1, right half is P2.
- Keyboard: WASD / arrow keys. **Space** (P1) / **Enter** (P2) uses your ability without letting go.
- Catch a ledge with your weapon and your jump comes back, so you can climb out.

## Modes
Play (one fight-setup screen: P1 & P3 on the left, P2 & P4 on the right (add/remove P2-P4), set each to Player or CPU Easy/Normal/Hard, customize each on its own half of the screen, pick mode and map), Testing Grounds (training dummy, try any weapon, no-cooldown and slow-mo toggles) and the Locker (spend coins on weapons, colors, hats and trails).

38 weapons (including movement weapons: Rocket Hammer, Grapple Gun, Warp Kunai, Pogo Staff, Jet Thruster) with a SUPER meter that supercharges your next ability, wall jumps, 26 maps (incl. walled arenas like the Dojo, Pinball, Skyscraper, Grand Canyon and Volcano Cave, low-gravity Moon Base, Warp Zone portals, a Factory with conveyors and a crusher, rising lava, breakable rope bridges, a highway with cars, crumbling ruins, the enclosed Kaboom Box arena with bomb rain, a spinning wheel, floating rafts, laser maps and Mushroom Grove's bounce pads), optional weapon-crate drops, parries, 100 HP health, end-of-match damage/combo stats, coins from KOs and wins.
