HOW TO PLAY

Joystick: the fixed stick in the bottom-right corner (P1 bottom-left in 2-player). Direction = movement and where your weapon points.

Melee weapons hurt just by hitting. The faster the blade moves, the harder it hits, so whip the stick around to swing.

Let go of the stick to use your weapon's ability. Each has a cooldown, shown on your card.

Hold up to jump. You get one jump, from the ground or in the air; it comes back when you land or grab a ledge. Push down to drop through the dashed see-through platforms. All other ground is solid.

Health: everyone has 100 HP. Hit 0 or fall off the map and you're KO'd.

Coins 🪙 come from KOs and wins. Spend them in the Locker (or when picking your fighter) to unlock weapons, colors, hats and trails. Testing Grounds lets you try any weapon for free.

2 Players: left half = P1 stick, right half = P2. Keyboard: WASD / arrows.
