Sword

STICK FIGHT

1v1 ragdoll stickman duels

VS CPU

HOW TO PLAY

Joystick: touch and drag anywhere. Direction = movement and where your weapon points.

Melee: your blade does damage just by hitting them. The faster it's moving, the harder it hits. Whip the stick around to swing.

Let go of the stick to use your weapon's ability (dash, slam, fireball, laser burst…). Each one has a cooldown, shown on your card.

Hold up to jump (it works mid-air too, whenever the cooldown's ready). Push down to drop through the see-through dashed platforms (all other ground is solid).

Damage % makes you fly further. Fly off the screen = KO. Everyone spawns with a random weapon, and crates drop new ones.

2 Players: left half = P1 stick, right half = P2. Keyboard: WASD / arrows.
